Trimify Admin — Button Design System

Deliverable 2: Keen As Mustard (KAM) button variations for approval.

Variation 1: Solid Primary (Elevated)

Used for main call-to-actions. Features a subtle lift and colored shadow on hover.

Variation 2: Soft Secondary (Subtle)

Used for secondary actions that don't need a heavy solid background. Deepens color on hover.

Variation 3: Crisp Outline

Used for alternative actions or cancels. Gains a slight background fill and darker border on hover.

Variation 4: Ghost (No Border/Bg)

Used for tertiary actions like datatable row actions or subtle text links. Shows background only on hover.

Variation 5: Destructive (Danger)

Used for deletion or dangerous actions. Includes red hover glow.
